About William Lu

I am a senior at the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign, pursuing a B.S. in Mathematics & Computer Science with an expected graduation in May 2027. I began my computer science studies at UW-Madison before transferring to UIUC, where I developed a deeper interest in software engineering, performance engineering, and artificial intelligence.

During my Summer 2026 software engineering internship with TikTok's Effect Core SDK team, I built an automated performance trace collection and reporting platform, designed an agentic performance attribution workflow, and established instrumentation from the SDK level down to individual JavaScript functions. Outside of code, I enjoy reading detective novels, photography, and video games — the last of which originally sparked my interest in game development and interactive technology. I am especially drawn to intelligent systems that are measurable, efficient, and grounded in real system behavior.
